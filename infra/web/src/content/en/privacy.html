2. Summary

  • You play without signing up: we don’t ask for your name, email or phone number. The game creates an anonymous identifier.
  • When you start, we ask for your age range (not your date of birth) and, depending on your age and country, your consent for analytics and personalized ads. Saying no is as easy as saying yes, and you can change it in Settings › “Privacy and data”.
  • If you say you are under 13, the game is locked and stops collecting usage data (section 6).
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, only after you state your age and make your consent choice. If you don’t accept personalized ads, ads are non-personalized; from 13 to 17 too, and in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland there are no ads; if you are under 13, there are no ads.
  • You can delete your account and your data in the game or on our website.

4. What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask for or store your name, email, phone number, address, photos, contacts, messages or precise location.
  • We don’t sell your personal data. If you are 18 or older and accept personalized ads, Google may use your device identifiers to show you ads based on your activity; some US state laws call this “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings › “Privacy and data”, which works as the “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” option. If you are under 18, we never share your data for personalized advertising and, in the US, we ask Google for restricted data processing.
  • There is no chat or free text from other players; the League displays the limited data described in section 3.
  • There are no random purchases: you see what you buy or win beforehand.

6. Age and minors

  • Tintalú is for people aged 13 and over and is not part of the Google Play Families program.
  • The first time you open the game we ask your age on a neutral screen, in three ranges: under 13, 13 to 17, and 18 or older. The answer is stored on your phone (if you accept analytics, it is also sent to Google Analytics for Firebase, as section 3 explains) and you can correct it once in Settings.
  • Under 13: the game is locked and asks you to have an adult install it. Crashlytics crash reports are turned off when you state your age. From then on, each time you open the game Crashlytics is not turned on and the Firebase Remote Config configuration is not downloaded (the game uses the one it already has); analytics is never turned on; neither the anonymous account, Google Play Games nor our server is used, purchases are not possible and ads never start.
  • 13 to 17: ads are non-personalized, and in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland there are no ads. For all ages we ask Google AdMob for ads suitable for all audiences (G), in the code and in the account settings. Purchases require a double confirmation in the game and Google Play authentication. In addition, in countries where the law requires parental permission below an age higher than 13 (14, 15, 16 or 18, depending on the country; for example, Germany, France, Spain, Colombia, Brazil, India or South Korea), analytics is not offered and stays off from 13 to 17. In Brazil, your parents or guardians can supervise and limit your purchases with Google’s tools (Family Link), and we never show random purchases.
  • If you think a child under 13 used the game, write to privacy@tintalu.com and we will delete their data.

8. International transfers

Parallext, LLC is based in the United States. Our server is in the São Paulo (Brazil) region of Amazon Web Services, and Google processes data in the United States and other countries. Your data may therefore leave your country, even if you live in Colombia, Peru, Mexico or Brazil. For data from the European Economic Area, our server in Brazil is covered by the European Commission’s adequacy decision for Brazil (2026). Transfers to the US rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework when the recipient is certified and, in any case, on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses included in the terms of Google (Firebase) and Amazon Web Services, with their addenda for the United Kingdom and Switzerland. For data from Brazil, Colombia, Peru, Mexico and other countries, we use those same contractual safeguards, as each country’s law allows (for example, art. 33 of the LGPD).

10. Your rights

You can request access to, correction, deletion and portability of your data and information about how it is used, object to processing, and withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

  • In the game: Settings › “Privacy and data” to change your choices and Settings › “Delete account and data” to delete your account.
  • By email: privacy@tintalu.com. We will reply within 10 business days at most (and never more than 15 calendar days in Brazil). If we can’t, we will tell you why and reply within the following 5 business days, and always within the period set by your country’s law (for example, one month in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, 15 days in Brazil or 45 days in California).
  • Brazil (LGPD, art. 18): you can also contact the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).
  • Colombia (Law 1581 of 2012): you can also contact the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio.
  • Peru (Law 29733): you can also contact the Autoridad Nacional de Protección de Datos Personales.
  • Mexico (LFPDPPP of 2025): you have ARCO rights (access, rectification, cancellation and opposition). If you disagree with our response, you can go to the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno (the Mexican Anti-Corruption and Good Governance Ministry), the authority for personal data held by private parties.
  • United States: depending on the state where you live, you can ask to know what data we hold, correct it, delete it, take it with you, and opt out of its sale, of “sharing” for behavioral advertising and of profiling, and appeal our response by writing to us again. We won’t discriminate against you for exercising these rights. The game is not directed to children under 13 and doesn’t collect their data: if someone states they are under 13, the game is locked (section 6). Before the age screen we only use identifiers for the game’s internal operations, as section 3 explains (“When you open the game”).

11. Deleting your account and data

There are two ways, with the same result:

  1. In the game: Settings › “Delete account and data”, with two confirmations. It also deletes the game data on your phone and asks for the copy of your saved game in Google Play Games to be deleted.
  2. On the web: on the account deletion page, with the code shown in Settings.

Your anonymous account, your data on our server and your Firebase Authentication user are deleted. The purchase ledger is kept, without your identifier, for tax purposes (section 9). For 2 hours we also keep a deleted-account mark, with your identifier only as a hash, so that nothing is written again in the name of the deleted account. Purchases are not refunded.

The copy in Google Play Games: the game asks for it to be deleted when you delete the account, and checks that it was deleted (deleting it again if needed) the next time you open the game with Play Games, if you are 13 or older. If you don’t open the game again, or you delete the account from the web (which cannot reach your Google account), delete it yourself from the Play Games app (Settings › delete this game’s data).

Deleting your account also removes your pseudonym and League data, including leaderboard rows we still retain. Score histograms do not identify players.

12. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored encrypted on our server. Features that grant value (purchases and in-app deletion) require the integrity check. The game doesn’t keep secret keys on the phone, and our logs don’t store your UID in clear text or your purchase receipts.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change it in a significant way, we will let you know in the game and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent again. We keep previous versions.
