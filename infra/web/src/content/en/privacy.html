2. Summary

  • You play without signing up: we don’t ask for your name, email or phone number. The game creates an anonymous identifier.
  • When you start, we ask for your age range (not your date of birth) and, depending on your age and country, your consent for analytics and personalized ads. Saying no is as easy as saying yes, and you can change it in Settings › “Privacy and data”.
  • If you say you are under 13, the game is locked and stops collecting usage data (section 6).
  • The game shows ads with AppLovin MAX, only after you state your age and make your consent choice. If you don’t accept personalized ads, ads are non-personalized; from 13 to 17 too [[ABOGADO: confirmar que se mantiene la regla de no personalizar a menores de 18 (biz_under18_no_personalization)]], and in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland there are no ads; if you are under 13, there are no ads.
  • You can delete your account and your data in the game or on our website.

4. What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask for or store your name, email, phone number, address, photos, contacts, messages or precise location.
  • We don’t sell your personal data. [[ABOGADO: redacción de «no vender ni compartir» según las leyes estatales de EE. UU.]]
  • There is no chat and no content from other players.
  • There are no random purchases: you see what you buy or win beforehand.

6. Age and minors

  • Tintalú is for people aged 13 and over and is not part of the Google Play Families program.
  • The first time you open the game we ask your age on a neutral screen, in three ranges: under 13, 13 to 17, and 18 or older. The answer stays only on your phone and you can correct it once in Settings.
  • Under 13: the game is locked and asks you to have an adult install it. Crashlytics crash reports are turned off when you state your age. From then on, each time you open the game Crashlytics is not turned on and the Firebase Remote Config configuration is not downloaded (the game uses the one it already has); analytics is never turned on; neither the anonymous account nor our server is used, purchases are not possible and ads never start.
  • 13 to 17: ads are non-personalized [[ABOGADO: confirmar que se mantiene la regla de no personalizar a menores de 18 (biz_under18_no_personalization)]], and in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland there are no ads. For all ages we ask the networks for ads suitable for all audiences (G), as far as each network’s settings allow [[DATO DEL DUEÑO: contenido máximo G configurado en cada red de anuncios]]. Purchases require a double confirmation in the game and Google Play authentication. [[ABOGADO: tratamiento de adolescentes por país (LGPD art. 14 y ECA Digital en Brasil)]]
  • If you think a child under 13 used the game, write to [[DATO DEL DUEÑO: correo de privacidad]] and we will delete their data.

10. Your rights

You can request access to, correction, deletion and portability of your data and information about how it is used, object to processing, and withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

  • In the game: Settings › “Privacy and data” to change your choices and Settings › “Delete account and data” to delete your account.
  • By email: [[DATO DEL DUEÑO: correo de privacidad]]. We will reply within [[ABOGADO: plazo de respuesta por ley]].
  • Brazil (LGPD, art. 18): you can also contact the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).
  • Colombia (Law 1581 of 2012): you can also contact the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio.
  • Peru (Law 29733): you can also contact the Autoridad Nacional de Protección de Datos Personales.
  • Mexico (LFPDPPP): you have ARCO rights (access, rectification, cancellation and opposition). [[ABOGADO: autoridad vigente en México y vía para acudir a ella]]
  • United States: [[ABOGADO: derechos según las leyes estatales de privacidad aplicables y COPPA (juego no dirigido a menores de 13)]]

11. Deleting your account and data

There are two ways, with the same result:

  1. In the game: Settings › “Delete account and data”, with two confirmations. It also deletes the game data on your phone.
  2. On the web: on the account deletion page, with the code shown in Settings.

Your anonymous account, your data on our server and your Firebase Authentication user are deleted. The purchase ledger is kept, without your identifier, for tax purposes (section 9). For 2 hours we also keep a deleted-account mark, with your identifier only as a hash, so that nothing is written again in the name of the deleted account. Purchases are not refunded.

12. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored encrypted on our server. Features that grant value (purchases and in-app deletion) require the integrity check. The game doesn’t keep secret keys on the phone, and our logs don’t store your UID in clear text or your purchase receipts.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change it in a significant way, we will let you know in the game and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent again. We keep previous versions.
