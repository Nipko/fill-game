Tintalú

Tintalú is a puzzle game for Android phones where you pour water from one cuia to another until each one holds a single color. Every color comes from a fruit or from the water of a river in the Amazon basin, and you travel up the river stop by stop, from the estuary to the source, at your own pace and with no time limit.

Tintalú is developed by Parallext, LLC (parallext.com). It is not on Google Play yet: once it is, the link will be here.

Publication on Google Play is planned through AUTOMATION AI S.A.S.
