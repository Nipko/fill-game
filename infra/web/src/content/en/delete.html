Delete your Tintalú account

You can delete your Tintalú account and its data at any time, free of charge. Tintalú is a game by Parallext, LLC.

In the game (the quickest way)

  1. Open Tintalú and go to Settings.
  2. Tap “Delete account and data”.
  3. Confirm twice.

This also deletes your saved game and the other game data stored on your phone.

From this page

If the game is no longer installed or you prefer to do it here, you need your account code. You can find it in Tintalú › Settings, on the line “Code to delete your account on the web”. It has 26 letters and numbers in groups separated by hyphens, for example 3HZ7-M2Q9-XK4T-8VB1-NC6D-RF0-WPE (this is only an example).

  1. Enter your code and tap “Continue”.
  2. Check the code and confirm with “Yes, delete” within the next 5 minutes.

Use the latest code you saw in Settings: when the game requests a new one (for example, after reinstalling it), the previous one stops working. We will never ask you for this code by email or phone.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous account, including in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your data on our server: the integrity check, your account code, verified ad rewards, your River League pseudonym, avatar and points, and the link between your purchases and your account.
  • For 2 hours a deleted-account mark remains, with your identifier only as a hash, so that nothing is written again in the account’s name; then it disappears.
  • In the game, also your progress, seeds, boosters and settings stored on the phone.

From this page we can’t reach your phone: the saved game on it is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings. If there is a copy in Google Play Games, delete it in the Play Games app (Settings › delete this game’s data).

What is kept

Your purchase history, with nothing that identifies you, for 7 years for tax purposes. What you bought can’t be recovered, and deleting your account does not by itself entitle you to a refund; this doesn’t limit your rights as a consumer or Google Play’s refund policy. Analytics data isn’t linked to your account and is deleted automatically according to its retention period. Backups of our server expire automatically within 35 days at most.

How long it takes

Right away: when you see “Done. Your account was deleted.” on this page, or “Your account and data have been deleted.” in the game, the deletion is complete.

If you don’t have the code

If the game is installed, open it with an internet connection and go to Settings: the code appears there, or you can delete the account directly. If the game is no longer installed, write to privacy@tintalu.com.
