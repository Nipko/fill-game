Tintalú

Sort the water of the Amazon

Pour water from one cuia to another until each one holds a single color. Simple and relaxing. Every color comes from a fruit or from the water of a river in the Amazon basin: açaí, aguaje, hog plum, camu camu…

Get it onGoogle Play Coming soonon iPhone

Free to play, with ads and optional purchases. Android 8.0 or later.

How to play

One tap, one pour. No timer and no move limit: play at your own pace.

  1. Tap a cuia then another one to pour the top water.
  2. Pour with logic only onto the same color or into an empty cuia.
  3. Seal every color you win when every cuia holds a single color.

Why you’ll love it

A calm puzzle with the soul of a river.

🌊

Travel up the Amazon

From the Estuary in Brazil through nine worlds in five countries. On the River Map, Capi’s canoe moves from stop to stop.

Fruit colors

A fair game

📅

Something new every day

The River Challenge brings a new board every day, and in the River Logbook you stamp a page a day and pick your reward.

🏆

River League

From level 41, earn points every week and compare yourself in the global and country rankings.
